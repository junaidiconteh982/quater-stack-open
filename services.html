A&G Digital Hub

Printing Services

  • Colour printing — D10 per page

How to Place an Order

  1. Bring your document on a phone, USB drive, or other supported device.
  2. Tell us the type and quantity of printing you need.
  3. Choose black-and-white or colour printing.
  4. Review the document before printing.
  5. Pay for the completed service and collect your documents.
