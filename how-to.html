How to Apply for a Gambian National Identity Card

Before You Go

Make sure you are ready to complete the Citizen Application Form. The form asks for personal information such as your name, date of birth, nationality, place of birth, occupation, and residential address.

What to Bring

Bring a suitable verification document. The application form lists documents such as a birth certificate, Seyfo's certificate, Alkalo's attestation, passport, Voter's ID, naturalization certificate, registration certificate, institutional IDs, and other identification documents.

Verification Documents

The verification document section also includes options such as a Social Security Number, Tax Identification Number (TIN), National Health Number, and Driver Licence Number.

At the Office

Complete the required sections of the application form with accurate information. The form includes sections for applicant contact details, parentage, spouse information where applicable, and other personal information.

Complete the Application

Check that the information you provide is correct. The form contains a declaration stating that the information and documents provided are genuine and correct.

Sign the Form

The applicant must provide a signature or thumbprint. The form also contains areas for the enrolment officer and authenticator to complete.

After You Finish

Review the completed application carefully and make sure all required information and documents have been provided. Keep your information accurate and report changes when necessary.
